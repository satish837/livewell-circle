A Lifestyle Offering by Max Estates

A curated ecosystem of partnership, privileges and experiences — for those who live well, on purpose.

More than a loyalty programme

This isn't a rewards app.
It's a circle you belong to.

As the world around us becomes increasingly tech-driven and high-speed, we find ourselves seeking what truly matters, connection.

At Max Estates, our well-being-first philosophy has allowed us to nurture a community of like-minded people, brought together by shared taste, values and vision.

The LiveWell Circle is a thoughtfully curated wellbeing ecosystem, a collection of partnerships, privileges and experiences designed to help you live well, every day.

As a member, you’ll enjoy complimentary wellness sessions, thoughtful community experiences and exclusive benefits across fitness, mindfulness, health, home, travel, dining and more.

Exclusively For

Homeowners across every Max Estates residence.

Max Estates Limited is the real estate development arm of the Max Group, building premium residential and commercial developments across Delhi NCR — guided by a philosophy of well-being, thoughtful design and community.
