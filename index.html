More than a loyalty programme

This isn't a rewards app.
It's a circle you belong to.

As the world grows louder and faster, we're building something quieter. The LiveWell Circle is a thoughtfully curated ecosystem of partnerships, privileges and experiences — handpicked, not aggregated — brought together by shared taste, values and vision. Every brand inside it was chosen to add a genuine dimension to everyday well-being, across fitness, mindfulness, health, home, travel and dining.

Our Philosophy

Real well-being
begins here.

As the world around us grows increasingly tech-driven and fast-paced, we find ourselves seeking what truly matters — connection. Well-being isn't bought in a single transaction. It's tended to, quietly, every day — much like this tree.
